Privacy Policy

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is GD Solutions e.U., Wilhelm-Gösser-Gasse 28, 8047 Graz, Austria, email: contact@gdsolutions.at.

2. Hosting and server log files

This website is provided via GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit the website, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the page requested and browser and operating system information, in order to deliver the website and ensure its security. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the website securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers to the USA are based on the adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which GitHub is certified. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Content delivery network (jsDelivr)

To display this website, the Bootstrap CSS framework is loaded from the jsDelivr content delivery network (Prospect One sp. z o.o., Poland). In the process, your IP address is transmitted to the operator. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in fast and consistent presentation of the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details: jsDelivr Privacy Policy.

4. Web analytics (Umami)

For statistical analysis of website usage we use the open-source software Umami, running on a self-operated server (analytics.jesterbox.org). No data is passed on to third parties. Umami does not use cookies and does not store any information on your device. It records the page visited, the referring website, browser, operating system, device type, screen size and country of origin. Your IP address is not stored; it is only used to generate an anonymous identifier that changes daily, so no conclusions can be drawn about your identity. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving our services (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Local storage (notice banner)

If you confirm the privacy notice on the German pages by clicking “OK”, an entry is set in your browser's local storage so that the notice is not shown again. This entry contains no personal data, is not transmitted to us and can be deleted at any time via your browser settings. This storage is strictly necessary for the service you requested (§ 165(3) Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021).

6. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, we process the information you provide (in particular your name, email address and the content of your message) to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is the performance of pre-contractual measures or a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The data is deleted once it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention obligations apply (e.g. seven years under § 132 Austrian Federal Fiscal Code, BAO).

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR). To exercise these rights, contact contact@gdsolutions.at.

If you believe that the processing of your data violates data protection law, you can lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority. In Austria, this is the Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at.
